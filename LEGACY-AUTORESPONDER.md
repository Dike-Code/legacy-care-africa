Three core emails for the **Legacy — Family Guide Leads** automation flow. Paste these into MailerLite's email composer.

**Brand voice:** Warm, grounded, plain-English, never clinical. Sound like a wise Kenyan auntie who is also a clinician. Refer to "your family." Sign every email from **Daniel & Peris Mwangi · Co-Founders**.

**Sender:** Legacy Care Africa <info@legacycareafrica.com>

>
> The pregnancy and postpartum chapters in the guide are written by the **Uzazi Wellness Care** team — the maternal-wellness arm of Legacy.
>
> Uzazi exists because of a quiet truth: Kenyan mothers are extraordinarily well-supported *during* labor and the first 24 hours after — and almost completely alone after that. The fourth trimester (the 12 weeks after birth) gets a single hospital follow-up, a baby clinic visit, and then silence.

So Uzazi sends a nurse home with you, supports your feeding, watches for postpartum complications, talks to your mother-in-law, and stays with you for as long as you need. ## Notes for the dev team

- The variable `{$first_name}` matches MailerLite's default custom-field syntax for the `name` field — confirm in your account's variable list (it may be `{name}` or `{first_name}` depending on field setup).
- The branched email selection (2A / 2B / 2C) is handled by the **conditional split** step in the automation, based on the `season_of_care` field value. See `MAILERLITE-SETUP.md`.
- The `season_of_care = learning` branch should *not* get email 2 — those subscribers go to the "Legacy — Newsletter Only" group and start receiving the monthly newsletter instead.
