Our Companies

Two subsidiaries. One promise of care.

Legacy Care Africa operates two registered Kenyan care companies — each independent in its operations and focused in its purpose, but united under shared values of dignity, warmth, and compassionate excellence.

Maternal & Birth Care · Subsidiary 01

Uzazi Wellness Care Limited

Gentle, expert care for every season of motherhood.

Uzazi (which means "motherhood" in Swahili) is Legacy Care Africa's maternal wellness subsidiary. From your first prenatal visit through postpartum recovery and beyond — Uzazi Wellness Care brings warm, professional support directly to your home. Experienced caregivers, lactation consultants, and doulas walk with mothers across every season of motherhood — with a deep understanding of every family we serve, and the same warm faces visiting you across your care plan.

Visit Uzazi Site ↗

Seven core care areas across every stage

Seven specialized service areas — each delivered in the comfort of your home, by trained care specialists who listen first.

  • Prenatal & Pregnancy CareRoutine check-ins, gentle education, and one-on-one support throughout pregnancy.
  • Birth PreparationChildbirth education, calm coping strategies, and preparation for partners.
  • Postnatal RecoveryGentle in-home support during the fourth trimester — rest, recovery, and reassurance.
  • Infant Feeding & LactationHands-on, judgment-free support for whatever feeding journey works for your family.
  • Women's WellnessHolistic care that extends beyond pregnancy — for women in every season of life.
  • Mental HealthConfidential, compassionate emotional support during one of life's biggest transitions.
  • Maternal NutritionPractical, culturally familiar nourishment guidance for every stage of motherhood.
Home & Recovery Care · Subsidiary 02

Nyumbani Support Solutions Limited

Compassion. Dignity. Support. Home.

Nyumbani means "home" in Swahili — and home is where our care lives. Dependable, dignified care, in the comfort of your home. From elderly companionship and post-discharge recovery to medical equipment delivered and set up in your home — Nyumbani Support Solutions brings dependable, dignified care to families across Kenya, backed by NCK-registered nurses, background-checked caregivers, and 24/7 on-call coordination.

Visit Nyumbani Site ↗

Seven focused services, built around what families really need

From the day after discharge through long-term elderly support, our care services are designed to keep recovery safe and dignity intact — without families carrying it all alone.

  • Elderly Care at HomeDaily companionship, bathing, dressing, mobility, meals, and medication reminders — delivered with patience and dignity.
  • Post-Discharge RecoveryStructured support after surgery, stroke, fractures, or hospital admission — nurse oversight, medication reconciliation, and safer recovery.
  • Skilled Nursing & OversightNCK-registered nurses for assessments, vitals, wound care, medication coordination, and escalation when conditions change.
  • Dementia & Frailty SupportGentle, dignity-centered support for seniors living with memory decline, frailty, fall risk, or reduced independence at home.
  • Child & Family Autism SupportIn-home autism support across the lifespan — routines, sensory and behavioural support, family respite, and therapy coordination.
  • Palliative & Comfort CareCompassionate care focused on comfort, pain relief, and dignity — for clients facing serious or life-limiting illness.
  • Equipment Rental BundlesHospital beds, wheelchairs, walkers, oxygen concentrators, commodes, and more — delivered, set up, and explained to your family.
One Group. One Standard.

Why we built two focused companies — not one general one

Maternal care and in-home recovery care call for different teams, different training pathways, and different rhythms of presence. By keeping our two companies focused — Uzazi walking with mothers through pregnancy, birth, and postpartum; Nyumbani caring for adults recovering at home, families navigating dementia or autism, and loved ones who need skilled nursing, equipment, or end-of-life support — every family gets the depth of experience their season of life deserves, under one shared promise of dignity, warmth, and care that feels like home.

Get Matched With the Right Company

Book a single intake call — our care team will listen to your family's situation and route you to the right subsidiary, or coordinate care across both if your family needs maternal and in-home support.
