About Legacy Care Africa

Born from love. Grounded in heritage.
Built for the families we serve.

Legacy Care Africa is a Pan-African healthcare group dedicated to bringing dignified, culturally grounded care to every African family — across two specialized subsidiaries and one shared promise.

Our Founding Story

A homecoming that became a healthcare movement

Legacy Care Africa began with a question two partners could not stop asking each other: Why is it so hard for African families to access the kind of compassionate, professional care they deserve — in their own homes, in their own languages, on their own terms?

Daniel Mwangi, working in the United States, saw firsthand how diaspora families struggled to arrange quality care for parents back home in Kenya. Peris Mwangi, on the ground in Nairobi, watched the same gap from the other side — mothers without skilled postpartum support, elders sent home from hospitals with no follow-up, families exhausted and improvising.

In 2022, they made the decision: instead of complaining about the gap, they would build the bridge themselves. Legacy Care Africa was incorporated as a Pan-African healthcare holding company — one that would launch focused, expert subsidiaries to meet each major need, while operating under shared values of dignity, cultural respect, and clinical excellence.

Today, Legacy Care Africa operates two registered subsidiaries — Uzazi Wellness Care for maternal and birth care, and Nyumbani Support Solutions for in-home and recovery care — with growth plans across East Africa.

Mission & Vision

What we are building, and why

Our Mission

To deliver compassionate, culturally grounded healthcare that bridges traditional African healing with evidence-based medical practice — ensuring every family we serve feels seen, honored, and well cared for.

Our Vision

A continent where geography, income, or cultural identity never determines the quality of care a family receives. Where every mother births with dignity, every elder recovers at home with comfort, and every family is supported in their own language and on their own terms.

Our Values

Four principles that shape every decision we make

Ubuntu

"I am because we are." Care does not happen in isolation — it happens within family, community, and culture. We honor the whole web.

Dignity First

Every patient deserves to be addressed by name, in their language, with respect for their story. Dignity is not an extra — it is the foundation.

Clinical Excellence

Warmth never substitutes for skill. Our caregivers and midwives are clinically trained, evidence-informed, and held to the highest professional standards.

Pan-African Pride

Built by Africans, for African families. We center African languages, foods, traditions, and healing wisdom — and pair them with modern medicine, not against it.

Our Journey

From a shared question to a Pan-African healthcare group

  1. 2022

    The vision takes root

    Daniel and Peris Mwangi commit to building a Pan-African healthcare group — one that places dignity, cultural respect, and clinical excellence at the center.

  2. 2023

    Uzazi Wellness Care launches

    The first subsidiary opens its doors, serving Kenyan mothers with prenatal monitoring, gentle birth support, postpartum wellness, and lactation guidance.

  3. 2024

    Nyumbani Support Solutions joins the family

    Our second subsidiary launches — bringing hospital-quality in-home care, post-discharge recovery, dementia support, and medical equipment rentals to families across Kenya.

  4. 2025

    Expanding our reach

    We grow our clinical team, add Autism & family support services at Nyumbani, and open a USA office to better serve the Kenyan diaspora arranging care for loved ones back home.

  5. 2026

    Across East Africa

    Our roadmap takes Legacy Care Africa beyond Kenya — with planned launches in Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda, while we continue to deepen our service quality at home.

Leadership
