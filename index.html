Our Companies

Two arms of care, one legacy

Legacy Care Africa operates two registered subsidiaries — each focused, each independent, each grounded in the same values of dignity, cultural respect, and clinical excellence.

Maternal & Birth Care

Uzazi Wellness Care

From preconception through postpartum recovery — gentle, expert in-home care for every mother. Seven specialized service areas including prenatal monitoring, birth support, postpartum wellness, perinatal mental health, and lactation guidance.

Learn About Uzazi Visit Uzazi Site ↗
Home & Recovery Care

Nyumbani Support Solutions

Hospital-quality care delivered at home. Seven services including elderly companionship, post-discharge recovery, skilled nursing, dementia and frailty support, child & family autism support, palliative comfort care, and medical equipment rental bundles.

Why Families Choose Us

Purpose-driven from the ground up

African-Led, Community-Centered

Every service is designed by Africans who understand the cultural, linguistic, and structural realities of healthcare on the continent. Not for us — by us.

Trauma-Informed Practice

Our caregivers are trained in empathic, trauma-informed care — meeting each patient exactly where they are, without rushing, dismissing, or judging.

Clinical Standards, Human Touch

Rigorous medical training combined with warmth. You will never feel like a number — and you will never be treated below the highest evidence-based standard.

Pan-African Reach

Headquartered in Kenya with a USA office serving the diaspora. Our growth roadmap takes us across East Africa — Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda are next.

What We Believe

Healthcare built by Africans, for Africans

We do not import a foreign model and translate it. We build from the cultural ground up — and bring the best of modern medicine to meet it.

Our Mission

To deliver compassionate, culturally grounded healthcare that bridges traditional African healing with evidence-based medical practice — ensuring every family we serve feels seen, honored, and well cared for.

Our Vision

A continent where geography, income, or cultural identity never determines the quality of care a family receives. Every mother births with dignity. Every elder recovers at home with comfort. Every family is supported in their language and on their terms.
